Questions people ask

How often should I service my car?

Most cars need a periodic service every 10,000 km or once a year, whichever comes first. If your car is older or you drive mostly in city traffic, every 6–8 months is safer.

Do you pick up and drop my car?

Yes. Turn on pickup & drop when you book and our partner collects the car in your chosen slot and brings it back once the service is done. You can also drop it at the garage yourself.

Will I be charged extra after the service starts?

Only if you approve it. If the mechanic finds something else, you get the item and price in the app first — the work happens only after you accept.

Which package should I choose?

Essential covers oil, filter and a 21-point check for routine upkeep. Premium Care adds AC, filters, wash and a 35-point check and is the one most people pick. Signature is the complete service with synthetic oil and 50+ checks.
