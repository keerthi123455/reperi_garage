Questions people ask

Ceramic or graphene — which is better?

Both protect the paint and add gloss. Graphene coatings tend to resist water spots better and run cooler on the surface; ceramic is the proven, slightly lower-cost option.

Does coating stop scratches?

It helps against light wash marks, but it isn't armour. For stone chips and real scratch protection, go for PPF.

Is the paint corrected before coating?

The paint must be decontaminated and polished first, otherwise the coating locks in the swirls. Paint correction is available if your paint needs it.
