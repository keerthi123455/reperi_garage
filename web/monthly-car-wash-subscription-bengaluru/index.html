Questions people ask

What's included every week?

6 water washes and 2 interior cleans a week, with daily updates in the app. Wednesdays are off.

Do I need to be there?

No — it's contact-free. The washer comes between 4 AM and 9 AM and you get an update in the app.

What if a wash is missed?

You get a free shampoo wash to make up for a missed day.

Can I renew easily?

Yes. The app reminds you near the end of the plan and you can renew in a couple of taps.
