Questions people ask

Do you wash the car at my home?

Yes — express and premium washes are done at your doorstep. The deeper spa and detailing packages can be done with pickup & drop.

Do you need water or power from me?

Usually not. If your parking has restrictions, mention it while booking and the washer will plan for it.

How often should I wash my car?

Weekly is ideal in Bengaluru's dust. If you want it clean every day, the monthly wash plan is cheaper than booking washes one by one.
