Questions people ask

What's the difference between polishing and paint correction?

Polishing adds gloss and hides very light marks. Paint correction machine-cuts the clear coat in stages to actually remove swirls and scratches, then finishes it to a high shine.

How long does a polish last?

A polish alone lasts a few weeks to a couple of months. To lock the shine in for longer, add a ceramic or graphene coating.

Can you remove scratches?

Light scratches and swirls usually come out with polishing or correction. Deeper scratches that reach the paint may need scratch control or panel paint work — you'll see the quote first.
