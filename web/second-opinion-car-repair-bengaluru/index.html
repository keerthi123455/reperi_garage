Questions people ask

When should I get a second opinion?

Whenever a quote is much higher than you expected, lists many parts at once, or recommends a major job like a clutch, gearbox or engine work. A short check can save a lot.

What do I need to share?

Book the visit and keep your garage or dealer quote handy — a photo is enough. The expert checks the car and the quote together.

Will Reperi pressure me to repair with them?

No. You get honest advice and a fair price estimate. Booking the repair with Reperi is optional.
