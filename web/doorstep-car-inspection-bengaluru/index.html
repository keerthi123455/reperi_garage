Questions people ask

What happens during a doorstep inspection?

The expert checks the problem you describe, takes a look over the rest of the car and shares photos and a clear quote for the repair in the app. Nothing is repaired during the visit.

Do I have to get the repair done with Reperi?

No. The inspection is a fixed price on its own. If you like the quote you can book the repair in the app, otherwise you keep the report.

How soon can the expert come?

Our aim is to reach you within 2 hours of booking during working hours. You'll see the expert's details and live status in the app.
