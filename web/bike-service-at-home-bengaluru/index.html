Questions people ask

How often should I service my bike?

Every 3,000–4,000 km or every 3–4 months for most commuter bikes and scooters. Follow your owner's manual for bigger bikes.

Why is the price based on CC?

Bigger engines need more oil, more parts and more time. Pick your bike's engine size and the price is fixed upfront.

Do you service Royal Enfield and KTM?

Yes — 350cc bikes are in the 201–350cc package and bigger bikes in the 351–500cc or 501cc+ packages.
