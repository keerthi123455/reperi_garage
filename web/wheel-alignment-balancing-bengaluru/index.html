Questions people ask

Alignment or balancing — what's the difference?

Alignment sets the angles of the wheels so the car drives straight. Balancing evens out the weight of each wheel so it doesn't vibrate. Most cars need both together.

How often should I get it done?

Every 8,000–10,000 km, after hitting a big pothole, after new tyres, or whenever the car pulls to one side.

Why is my steering wheel shaking?

Vibration at speed is usually an unbalanced wheel. If the car also pulls to a side, alignment is off too.
