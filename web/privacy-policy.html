Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Reperi Garage ("we", "our", "us") provides vehicle service booking and related services through our mobile app and website (together, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name and email address, used to create and manage your account.
  • Vehicle information: vehicle make and model, used to provide accurate service bookings.
  • Location data: your device location, used to auto-detect your service/pickup address when you book any service, and to dispatch help when you request Roadside Assistance. You control this permission via your device settings (Settings → Apps → Reperi Garage → Permissions → Location). You can revoke it at any time; the app continues to function normally without it — you can enter your address manually instead — but Roadside Assistance dispatch will not be available until permission is re-granted.
  • Photos: optionally, photos of your vehicle that you choose to upload (e.g. to our AI Advisor or for inspection/booking updates), used to help recommend the right service.
  • Identity & vehicle documents: for insurance claims, you may upload your RC copy, driving license, Aadhaar, PAN, insurance copy, and damage photos. These are used solely to process your claim with the insurer/garage partner and are not used for any other purpose.
  • Payment information: when you make a payment, it is processed directly by our payment partner, Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details — we only store the transaction status and a reference ID to confirm your booking.
  • Booking and service history: details of services booked, scheduled, and completed through the app.
  • Fleet account data: for business/fleet accounts, company name and associated vehicle and booking records.
  • Push notification preferences: your device identifier and notification preferences, managed by OneSignal to deliver booking updates and service alerts. You can opt out of notifications in your device settings or app preferences.
  • AI Advisor conversations: if you use the AI Advisor, the text you type and any photo you choose to attach are sent to our AI provider to generate a response. We only send your message, your vehicle's make/model, and any photo you attach — never your name, phone number, or address.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and vehicle profile
  • To process and fulfill service bookings and payments
  • To auto-detect your service/pickup address and dispatch roadside assistance when requested
  • To provide AI-based service recommendations based on photos or descriptions you submit
  • To process insurance claims through our garage/insurer partners
  • To communicate booking confirmations, updates, and support responses via push notifications
  • To improve and maintain the Service

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the following third parties, solely to operate the Service:

  • Razorpay — to process payments securely
  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider, which stores your account, vehicle, and booking data on our behalf
  • OneSignal — to send push notifications about booking updates and service alerts. You can opt out of notifications in your device settings or app preferences.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — to generate AI Advisor responses when you describe an issue or upload a photo. Only your message text, vehicle make/model, and any photo you choose to attach are sent — never your name, phone, or address.

Data Retention

We retain your account and booking information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service and comply with legal obligations.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the details below. You can delete your account directly from the app's Profile screen, which permanently removes your account, vehicle, booking, address, insurance claim, and AI report data, or if you no longer have the app installed, visit our Account Deletion page to request deletion by email.

Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for use by adults capable of owning or operating a vehicle. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
