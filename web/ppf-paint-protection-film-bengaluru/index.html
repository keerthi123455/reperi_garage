Questions people ask

PPF or ceramic coating?

PPF physically protects against chips and scratches; ceramic adds gloss and easy cleaning. Many owners do PPF first and a coating on top.

Can PPF be removed later?

Yes, a good-quality film can be removed without harming factory paint.

How much does full-body PPF cost?

It depends on the car's size and the film. You'll see the price for your car in the app or get a quote before anything is done.
