Questions people ask

What does an OBD scan tell me?

It reads the fault codes stored by your car's computer and live data from sensors like oxygen, coolant temperature and throttle. That points to the system that's failing — engine, ignition, fuel, emissions or electrical.

My check engine light is on. Is it safe to drive?

A steady light usually means get it checked soon. A flashing light means a serious misfire — drive gently and get it scanned as soon as possible to avoid damaging the catalytic converter.

Can the scan be done at my home?

Yes. Add the OBD scan to a Doorstep Car Inspection and our expert scans the car at your parking spot.
