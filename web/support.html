Common Topics

  • Booking a service — Open the app, choose a service category (Servicing, Washing, Painting, Wheel Management, Detailing, etc.), select a package, and confirm your booking.
  • Tracking your booking — Go to "My Bookings" to see live status updates and photo updates of your vehicle.
  • Payments — We support secure online payments (cards, UPI, and more) as well as cash on delivery.
  • Insurance claims — File and track your claim directly from the app under the Insurance section.
  • Account deletion — Go to Profile → Delete Account in the app, or email us to request deletion. See our Privacy Policy for details.
  • Roadside assistance — Available directly from the app for urgent help.
