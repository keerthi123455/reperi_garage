Questions people ask

Why is my car AC not cooling?

The usual reasons are low refrigerant gas (often from a small leak), a clogged cabin filter, a dirty condenser or a weak compressor. An AC service checks each of these and tells you which one it is.

How often does a car AC need servicing?

Once a year, ideally before summer. Get it checked sooner if cooling drops, the AC smells or you hear new noises when it's on.

Does AC service include gas refill?

Yes — the AC packages include a gas top-up/refill with a leak and cooling-efficiency check. If a part like the compressor needs replacing, you'll see the price in the app before anything is done.
